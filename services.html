Logistics & Delivery

Secure Delivery

Expert Setup

Precision Installation

Professional installation guarantees your pool table is assembled correctly, positioned perfectly, and prepared for smooth, consistent play. Our certified team installs with strict attention to alignment, slate stability, and cloth tension.

Structural Assembly

Major components are assembled carefully matching the manufacturer's structural blueprints.

Leveling Mastery

The table is precisely positioned and leveled on all axes for an absolutely true playing surface.

Secure Fittings

We utilize specialized tools to secure the table, ensuring all joints and slates are tightly fitted.

Play-Ready Check

Post-installation, a final comprehensive check ensures stability and tournament-ready status.

Installation Fee: Charged separately based on the specific labour required to assemble, position, level, and stretch the cloth for your chosen table model.
