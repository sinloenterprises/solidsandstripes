Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide islandwide delivery?
Yes, we provide secure delivery and professional installation services all across Sri Lanka.
How long does installation take?
A standard professional installation usually takes between 2 to 4 hours, ensuring the table is perfectly leveled and the felt is flawlessly fitted.
What warranty do you offer?
All our premium pool tables come with a comprehensive warranty covering structural defects to give you total peace of mind.
Do you offer repair services for old tables?
Absolutely! We specialize in felt replacement, cushion repairs, and table leveling for any brand of pool table.
What room size do I need?
As a general rule, you need at least 5 feet of clear space around the table for comfortable cueing. Contact us with your room dimensions for expert advice.
