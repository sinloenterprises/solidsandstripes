Our Story

Founded in 2005, Solids and Stripes Pool Table services has been dedicated to bringing high-quality pool tables to enthusiasts in Sri Lanka. Our journey began with a passion for the game and a commitment to craftsmanship.

Over the years, we have grown from a small local business to a respected name in the industry, known for our exceptional products and outstanding customer service. At Solids and Stripes, we take pride in offering custom-made tables that combine elegance, durability, and exceptional playability.
